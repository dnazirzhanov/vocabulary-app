# 0045 — First run: two small questions; login first

**Status:** Accepted (the owner's decision after going through the real sign-up, 4 November 2026). Replaces
the first-run screens of ADR 0010 and 0039.

## Decision
- **The page opens on login, every time,** even from an invite link. Sign-up is one tap away. New visitors see
  it too, so it says «Вход», not «С возвращением».
- **A new password is typed twice,** at sign-up and after a reset. «Показать» stays.
- **«Выйти» is also on the main screen,** quietly in the top bar of «Мои колоды». It also stays in ⚙.
- **First run is two questions, one a screen, with no picture or explanation:** how many new words a day (3, 5,
  10 or 20; tapping one moves on), and on which days (with «Начать»).

## Consequences
- **The course goal («1 300 слов ≈ 80%») is no longer shown anywhere.** ADR 0010's reason for it was removing
  uncertainty about what the course gets a learner. If learners ask, it fits on the course page. The data and
  its source stay in the content.
- First run no longer spells out what a big pace costs in minutes, or offers the warm-up as a choice. The warm-up
  stays on by default, and the deck's page says so. Other amounts are set on a deck's page.
- Nothing at first run says friends in a group see the promise; the group's page does.
